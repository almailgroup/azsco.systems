How we work

Supply is the middle step, not the whole job

Equipment bought without a survey tends to be the wrong equipment, and equipment installed without maintenance stops working quietly. We quote all three together.

  1. 01

    Survey

    We walk the site, find the blind spots and the ways in, and agree what actually needs covering before anything is priced.

  2. 02

    Specify & quote

    You get a drawing, a device schedule and one price covering equipment, installation and the first year of maintenance.

  3. 03

    Install & commission

    Our own technicians cable, mount and configure the system, then hand it over tested with your staff trained on it.

  4. 04

    Maintain & monitor

    Scheduled service visits, a call-out number that answers, and optional connection to our 24/7 Central Operations Room.
