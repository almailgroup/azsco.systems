ABOUT AZSCO

A Kuwaiti security company that both installs the system and answers it.

Our story

Equipment and the people behind it

AZSCO Security Services Company — formerly Almail Zone Security Services — was established in Kuwait in 2014. It began as a manpower company: licensed, trained and supervised officers guarding facilities across the country.

Security systems followed, because the two problems are one problem. A camera nobody watches records an incident for later; a guard without cameras covers one place at a time. We now supply and install the systems as well as staffing the sites, and our Central Operations Room watches both.

That is the difference between us and a hardware supplier: we are the people the alarm reaches. A system we install can report to a room that is staffed at three in the morning, and to a patrol that can be at the site shortly after.

Objective

To protect what our clients have built — their premises, their people and their property — with systems that are specified honestly and maintained properly.

Vision

To be the security partner Kuwaiti organisations call first, trusted for the advice as much as for the equipment.

Mission

To deliver security systems and manpower to a consistent standard, through trained people, documented processes and equipment we are prepared to stand behind.

Why buy the system from us

What a supplier alone cannot offer

The alarm reaches a staffed room

Our Central Operations Room is manned around the clock, so a system we install can report to people rather than to an empty inbox.

A patrol can be sent

Because we also provide the manpower, a confirmed alarm can put a patrol on site instead of ending as a notification on someone's phone.

One contract for the whole system

Survey, equipment, installation, commissioning and maintenance come from one company, so there is nobody to blame it on when something stops working.

Certified and accountable

ISO 9001:2015 certified and licensed in Kuwait, with the documentation a government or banking client is required to ask for.
